I am a second-year Ph.D. student at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Pheng-Ann Heng. Previously, I have spent wonderful times as a research intern at the Qwen Team, Alibaba Group and Tencent.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs), Multimodal Understanding, and Agentic Systems. My long-term goal is to build intelligent systems that perceive the real world, reason about it, and act on their own decisions.

I am always open to communication and collaboration. Please feel free to get in touch.
