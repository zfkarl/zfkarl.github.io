Fan Zhang (章帆)

CSE Ph.D. Student @ The Chinese University of Hong Kong

I'm currently a first-year Ph.D. student at Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Pheng-Ann Heng. Previously, I gained valuable experience as a research intern at Alibaba Group and Tencent.

My research interests include Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multimodal Understanding, with an emphasis on post-training, reasoning, evaluation, agents, and their applications.
